We know what FAIR is – but how do we do it?

Policies and guidance are separated from everyday research practice by a missing middle layer of practical, reusable materials. This project builds that bridge.

ACCESS

CULTURAL

PLAYBOOK 1

How to approach FAIRification

Activity cards and facilitator scripts. Practical translation from existing resources such as RDMkit, FAIRsharing, FAIR Cookbook, WorkflowHub, Galaxy, and much more from the entire community.

  • Activity instructions (FAIR games, practical guidance translation)
  • Template slide decks & facilitator scripts
  • Placeholders for RPO-specific policies, repositories, contacts
PLAYBOOK 2

How to communicate & advocate FAIR

Scripts and talking points for researchers, PIs and decision-makers, plus FAIR games for students and early-career researchers – gamified, inclusive, neurodivergent-friendly.

  • Drop-in games for undergrad / MSc / PhD audiences
  • Scripted talking points for PIs & decision-makers
  • Local-adaptation placeholders & case summaries

Open Access · CC-BY 4.0 · indexed via RDMkit, FAIR Cookbook, TeSS, FAIRsharing & Zenodo

Playbooks v3

Final, openly published via RDMkit, FAIR Cookbook, TeSS, Zenodo & FAIRsharing.

●One-day, in-person events co-delivered with local teams – builds facilitator capacity, embeds local systems, prevents fly-in / fly-out delivery.
●Morning · drop-in FAIR games + scripted talking points. Afternoon · structured FAIRification workshop on local datatypes.
●Light pre / post baseline captures changes in FAIR provision, capacity and routes to implementation – early evidence of culture change.
